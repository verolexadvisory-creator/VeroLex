Corporate Law

A Solid Foundation for Your Business

Corporate law is the foundation of any business. We provide professional assistance at all stages, from company registration to resolving complex corporate disputes.

§ 01

Company Formation

Establishment of new companies, opening branches, preparation of all legal documents, and registration with state authorities.

§ 02

Corporate Governance

Shareholder agreements, board of directors decisions, shareholder meetings, and management body activities.

§ 03

Contracts Drafting

Drafting, editing, and legal advice on various contracts (sales, services, partnership, etc.).

§ 04

Reorganization

Legal services for company mergers, divisions, reorganization, and liquidation.

§ 05

Legal Expertise

Legal review of existing contracts, identification of deficiencies, and advice on their elimination.

§ 06

Dispute Resolution

Legal assistance in resolving disputes between shareholders, directors, and management bodies.

Legal basis

The Civil Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan, the laws on Limited and Additional Liability Companies and on Joint-Stock Companies and Protection of Shareholders' Rights, and the Law on Guarantees of Freedom of Entrepreneurial Activity.
