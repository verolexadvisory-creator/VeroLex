Licenses and Permits

Easy Cooperation with State Bodies

Entrust complex bureaucratic processes to us. We will assist you at all stages of licensing and permitting processes.

§ 01

Obtaining a License

Full legal service for obtaining licenses for various activities. Communication with state bodies and document preparation.

§ 02

Permits

Obtaining permits for construction, import-export, healthcare, and other sectors. We support all processes.

§ 03

License Renewal

Renewing existing licenses and permits and extending their terms. Reminder service for timely renewal.

Legal basis

Law No. ZRU-701 of 14 July 2021 on Licensing, Permit and Notification Procedures, Presidential Decree No. PF-6044 of 24 August 2020 and the relevant unified regulations of the Cabinet of Ministers.
